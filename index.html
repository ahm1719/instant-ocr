Tips for best OCR results

  • Use clear, sharp images with good lighting and high contrast.
  • Crop away borders/backgrounds; rotate to upright orientation.
  • Pick the correct Language (Arabic ≠ English).
  • For multi-column documents, try scanning each column separately.
  • If results seem slow, your browser may be throttling — try closing heavy tabs.
